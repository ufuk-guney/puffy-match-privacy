This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights when you use the Puffy Match mobile application ("Game"). By downloading and using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Automatically Collected Information

The Game and the third-party SDKs it uses may automatically collect the following information:

  • Device model, OS version, language and region
  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
  • In-game events (level, session duration, taps, crash reports)
  • Anonymous device identifiers for analytics

1.2 Information You Provide

The Game does not require account registration. If you contact us for support, we receive the information you share in that message (email address, content).

1.3 Purchase Information

In-App Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store. We do not have access to your credit card or payment details. We only receive an anonymous transaction confirmation from Apple.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve the Game experience
  • To detect bugs and analyze crash reports

  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following third-party services. Each provider's own privacy policy applies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) — Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports. Policy
  • Apple App Store / In-App Purchase — Payment processing. Policy

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / Apple Kids)

The Game is published in the "All Ages / Families" category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

  • The Game does not display any advertisements.
  • Third-party analytics SDKs operate in COPPA-compliant mode only.

Anonymous analytics are retained for up to 14 months. Support emails are deleted within a reasonable period after the issue is resolved.
